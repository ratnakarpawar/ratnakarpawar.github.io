Multidimensional Difference Awareness Benchmark

A benchmark for when a rule should treat people differently, and when it shouldn't. 817 items in paired contrasts, built from verbatim institutional sources across 10 jurisdictions, including the hard cases where a demographic axis is a legitimate input. The sharpest finding comes from rules that changed their minds: cite a superseded guideline and models apply today's version instead. On sex-based insurance pricing, accuracy on the cited old rule falls to 0.22. Human raters fail on different items than models do, without the models' systematic asymmetry, which is what makes routing between them worth the cost. Working paper → Dataset → Version-flip set →
